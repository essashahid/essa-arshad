## The problem

Agents that send email or change records are easy to demo and hard to trust. A team needs a setup where every outbound action waits for a person, and every step is logged. {/* TEMP */}

## What I built

1. Deploy OpenClaw with a read-only inbox tool and a draft-only send tool {/* TEMP */}
2. Hold every outbound message in an approval queue {/* TEMP */}
3. Log each step with a timestamp and trace every run {/* TEMP */}

## Why these settings

**Approve before send.** Nothing leaves until a person clicks Approve. {/* TEMP */}
