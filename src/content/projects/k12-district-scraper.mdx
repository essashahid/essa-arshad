## The problem

District websites are inconsistent, slow and often JavaScript-heavy. And a wrong email is worse than a missing one, because someone sends outreach to it.

## What I built

1. Load districts from the NCES list
2. Fetch with requests, fall back to Playwright <span class="text-muted">· robots.txt, rate limits, cache</span>
3. Extract, dedupe and attach a source URL to each field
4. Grade confidence; send low rows to a review queue
5. Export CSV and an HTML QA report

## Why these settings

**Source on every field.** Anyone can check a value in one click.

**Resumable runs.** A crash at row 312 restarts at row 312.

**50 before 500.** A small run proved grading before the full one.
