## The problem

Listing sites bury well-designed homes under thousands of ordinary ones. And a home is only half the decision, because the neighborhood decides how it is to live there. {/* TEMP */}

## What I built

1. City and neighborhood browsing across six US cities
2. Buy and rent views with map search
3. A compare tool for up to 4 homes
4. A mortgage calculator <span class="text-muted">· taxes, insurance, HOA</span>
5. Saved homes and consultation requests
